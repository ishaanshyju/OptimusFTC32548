01 / ABOUT OPTIMUS

More than a robot.
A team that builds.

Based in Round Rock, Texas, Optimus FTC 32548 is one of Round Rock High School’s robotics teams. We bring creativity, engineering, and teamwork together to take on the challenges of FIRST Tech Challenge.

From an early sketch to a working mechanism, we learn by doing. Every design, line of code, and test gives us a new way to grow together.
